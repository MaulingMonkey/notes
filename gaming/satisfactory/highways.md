# Truck Highways
(Measurements are generally in 8x8x4m foundation blocks.)

## One-Way Roads
Consider using them.  I keep doing bidirectional roads and I hate 'em.  So many potentially busy intersections, etc.
An automated truck can sanely fit on 1 foundation wide roads.  A player driven truck can sanely fit on 1.5 foundation wide roads.
If you don't mind belts overhanging the edge, truck stations will only add a single foundation's worth of width to the sides they're used on.
If you *do* mind, add 1.5 foundations.  Or 2.0 if you want rotated storage containers.

## U-Turns
A truck has a U-turn circumference of about 2.125 foundations based on the centerline, or 3.125 based on the outer radius of the truck.
You can *almost* handle this nicely on a 3-wide truck foundation.
But if you do my preferred style of `o---o` style roads, with concrete pillars as curbs on the ends, the truck tires lift up on the turn even if you nudge the route out only a half-step.
The wheels don't even touch the curb visually, the hitboxes are just too big.
Won't disqualify it as an option in a pinch, but this makes me prefer 3.5 or 4.0 wide bidirectional roads, or one-way roads.

## Two-Way Roads
Four foundation wide roads feel a bit extravagant.
However, this keeps a nice even centerline for foundation patterns, and you can use the middle area for truck stations or narrow ramps.
