# Early Roadmap

-   Unlock Tier 1: Base Building (Ramps)
    -   200x Concrete
    -   100x Iron Plate
    -   100x Iron Rod
-   Unlock Tier 1: Field Research (Map, +3 Inventory, [MAM])
    -   300x Wire
    -   300x Screws
    -   100x Iron Plates
-   Unlock Tier 2: Obstacle Clearing (+3 Inventory, Chainsaw)
    -   500x Screws
    -   100x Cable
    -   100x Concrete
-   Construct Chainsaw
    -   160x Screws
    -   15x Cable
    -   25x Rods
    -   5x Reinforced Iron Plate
-   Obtain Parachute
    -   60x Mycelia (30 for [MAM] Unlocks, 30 for Fabric)
    -   30x Fabric (10 for [MAM] Unlock, 20 for Parachute itself)
-   Raid Doggo Cave for Bladerunners, Xenobasher, etc.
-   Raid Crash Sites for Coupon Bait
