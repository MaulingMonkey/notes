# Corporation

Benefits include:
-   Corp Chat
-   Shared Hangers
-   Shared Wallet
-   Style
-   Tax Evasion

## Chat > `Corp` > Own Portrait >
-   **CEO Only:** `⚠️ Resign as CEO?` > `Confirm Resign as CEO`
-   **Non CEO:** `⚠️ Quit Corporation?` > `...`

## `Neocom` > `Corporation` > `Corporation` Tab > `General` > `My Corporation` >
-   Edit Directly:
    -   `Friendly Fire`
    -   `Structure Transfers`
    -   `Corporation Mail`
-   Edit through `Edit Details`:
    -   `ISK Tax Rate`
    -   `LP Tax Rate`
    -   `URL`
    -   `Description`
    -   `Member Limit` (via `Update With My Skills`)
-   Edit hanger/wallet names through `Divisions` **(CEO Only)**.  I name mine:
    1.  `Drop` (Tags, NPC Loot, etc.)
    2.  `Ships` (also deployables like Mobile Depots, MTUs, Containers)
    3.  `Consumables` (drones, ammo, missiles, charges, skills, boosters, fillaments)
    4.  `Equipment` (slottables, rigs)
    5.  `Cosmetics`
    6.  `Junk`
    7.  `Industry` (blueprints, ore, minerals, salvage, datacores, scrap, R.A.M.s, ...)
-   Pay `Dividends` to shareolders **(CEO Only?)**

## `Neocom` > `Corporation` > `Administration` Tab >
-   `Members` > `Title Management` >
    -   Cycle through all the combinations of `View` x `Type` for the role you want to configure
    -   Only necessary if you're not granting `Director`
-   `Members` > `Role Management` >
    -   `Titles` >
        -   Grant roles to members that cover *most* settings here
    -   `Permissions` >
        -   **CEO Only:** Grant `Director` here
        -   Optionally, select `Base` from offices here
