## Alpha Ship Fits

I'd lean towards one of:

| ISK       | m3/min    |    / 3.12 | Description           | Notes         |

|   237,000 |       312 |      100% | Disposable Venture    | Use for "high risk" mining.  Maybe add a shield extenders and an afterburner to survive a gate camp volley?
| 3,500,000 |       372 |      119% | T1 Pioneer            | Tide you over to T2.
| 7,000,000 |       456 |      146% | T2 Pioneer            | 30x

The disposable venture can only fly 2 light drones to the pioneer's 5, and can only carry 5k ore instead of 8k.
That said, it's *significantly* cheaper, so consider it for "high risk" mining.
Ignore the *Consortium Issue* variants: they are lots of bling for little gain (a little more ore hold, +10 salvaging drone bonus.)
Ignore the *Preserverence*: it's bonuses are for ice mining, which requires Omega, even if it can technically be flow by Alphas.

### 237,000 ISK &mdash; Disposable Venture (Mining Frigate)

Cheap.  Disposable.  Built in warp core stabilization.  Use for high risk mining.

### 3,500,000 - 7,000,000 &mdash; Pioneer (Mining Destroyer)

You lose warp core stabilization and pay 15-30x the price for:
-   +19-46% more mining yield
-   +150% more drones in flight (2 -> 5 light drones)
-   +60% larger ore hold (5,000 m3 -> 8,000 m3)

Tradeoffs for low/nullsec:
-   More difficulty fleeing from players
-   Better able to fight off belt rats
-   Larger loss when you inevitably get blown up
