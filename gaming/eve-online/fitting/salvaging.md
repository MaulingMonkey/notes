# EVE Online: Salvaging

I tend to drop and bookmark *Mobile Tractor Unit*s to gather wrecks in one place, then come back later in a T1 frigate to sweep up.
Destroyers are overkill unless you're an Omega account with access to higher levels of multi-targeting.
MTUs can be destroyed without CONCORD retaliation, even in high security space, and they're 10M ISK a pop, so buyer beware.

Said T1 frigates typically benefit from a cap battery to avoid running out of energy while salvaging.
A 5MN MWD is also appropriate.
The rest I tend to put into tank or agility.
With all your high slots filled with salvagers, your only DPS will be drones.
The *Imicus* can fly 4 light drones at a time intead of 3, so that might be worth considering for dps?
Otherwise, just fit for whatever frigate you have highest rank.
